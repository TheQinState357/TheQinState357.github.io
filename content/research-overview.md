### Assessing provider reliability

We compare the energy received in each transfer with the amount predicted under the same conditions. Across repeated transfers, we use these differences to estimate how likely a provider is to deliver the requested energy within the agreed interval. This estimate guides two decisions: which provider to select and how much energy to reserve for the request. We are also exploring how to combine offers when no single provider is likely to meet the request in full.

### Energy delivery under changing conditions

When two devices move together, their separation and alignment may remain unchanged. We study how changes in their relative position and orientation affect energy loss during a transfer. We also examine consecutive deliveries. A provider may finish one transfer with enough charge for the next while its battery is still warm. We test whether measuring battery temperature before the next transfer improves our estimate of fulfilment beyond what we know from remaining charge, recent activity and past deliveries.
