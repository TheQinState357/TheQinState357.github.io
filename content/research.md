## Choosing an energy provider
In a library or café, several devices may offer spare battery energy. Their offers describe how much they can share and for how long. A service coordinator must work out which offers can meet a request before the consumer leaves. I study what previous deliveries tell us about a provider and how that evidence can inform service selection and composition.

## Consecutive services
A phone that has just finished charging another device may still have enough energy for its next request. But does it have enough time to deliver that energy if charging slows or stops? I study the role of the provider’s starting battery temperature in consecutive transfers.

## Relative movement
Two devices can move together without changing their alignment. They can also shift relative to each other, changing the conditions of the transfer. I investigate whether distinguishing these cases improves predictions of how much energy the consumer will receive.
