I’m a PhD student in the School of Computer Science at the University of Sydney. I work in the Sensors, Clouds and Services Lab under the supervision of [Professor Athman Bouguettaya](http://scslab.net/our-team/athman-bouguettaya/) and [Associate Professor Jiangshan Yu](https://jiangshanyu.github.io/index.html).

When several nearby devices offer to charge a phone wirelessly, which one can deliver the energy it promises before the user leaves? This question is at the centre of my research on crowdsourced energy services.
